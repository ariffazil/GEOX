# GEOX — Repo Role Boundary

> Role: Earth Stewardship Topology Engine
> Anti-Sink Responsibility: Detect extraction-only resource logic, feedback breaks

---

## Invariant Role

GEOX is the **Earth intelligence and geoscience coprocessor** of the federation. It:
- Processes petrophysical, geospatial, and Earth system data
- Detects resource chokepoints and infrastructure fragility
- Evaluates extraction vs. stewardship topology
- Provides evidence for Earth system decisions

## Must Never Become

- A final policy judge or seal authority (that is arifOS)
- An economic allocator (that is WEALTH)
- A forge/mutation engine (that is A-FORGE)

## Core Question

> Does this Earth intelligence serve stewardship or extraction only?

## Anti-Sink Diagnostics

GEOX should eventually implement:
- Resource chokepoint detection (where is a single point of control?)
- Extraction vs. stewardship scoring per project/region
- Ecological feedback integrity (is the system learning from consequences?)
- Community benefit topology (who benefits vs. who bears cost?)
- Social licence risk alongside physical system risk
