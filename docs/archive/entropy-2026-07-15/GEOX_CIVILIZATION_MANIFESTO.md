**Sovereign:** Arif Fazil

> GEOX is the layer that turns Earth from background scenery into computable, governable, auditable intelligence.

## The Core Concept
Civilization cannot act on “Earth” directly. It acts through abstractions: maps, borders, land titles, roads, ports, pipelines, power grids, weather models, risk maps, population density, resource basins, flood zones, seismic zones, carbon maps, supply networks.

GEOX converts messy Earth reality into governed objects: asset, location, exposure, risk, route, jurisdiction, resource, dependency, vulnerability, opportunity.

This allows arifOS to reason:
- What is affected?
- Who has authority?
- What depends on this?
- What is the blast radius?
- What evidence supports this?
- What action is reversible?
- What must be escalated to Arif?

GEOX is not just geospatial. It is **civilization state compression**.

## The Three A's of GEOX

### 1. Abstraction (Earth becomes computable)
GEOX converts Earth into structured intelligence.

`EarthClaim` Schema:
- `subject`: what/where
- `claim`: what is asserted
- `source`: who/what observed it
- `timestamp`: when observed
- `freshness`: current/stale/expired
- `confidence`: low/medium/high
- `evidence_type`: sensor/report/model/inference
- `jurisdiction`: authority context
- `risk`: consequence if wrong
- `reversibility`: can action be undone?

### 2. Attestation (Earth claims must be proven)
If Earth claims are false, people die, money disappears, governments fail. GEOX requires strong Earth attestation.
No Earth claim should be treated as truth unless its source, time, and confidence are known.
**The key law:** No map without witness. No action without provenance.

## The Seven Layers of Civilization Intelligence

1. **Earth Identity Layer**: Every place/asset/event needs stable identity (`asset_id`, coordinate, jurisdiction, owner, data source, last verified, dependency links).
2. **Freshness Layer**: Freshness tiers (fresh, recent, stale, expired, unknown). Stale data is dangerous.
3. **Provenance Layer**: Distinguish observed fact, official record, human report, model inference, LLM interpretation, abductive hypothesis. A model-generated guess must never wear the uniform of sensor truth.
4. **Consequence Layer**: Every geospatial claim must carry blast radius (e.g., bridge safety wrong vs restaurant location wrong). Consequence-aware routing.
5. **Jurisdiction Layer**: Who has authority here? (country, state, tribal law, maritime zone, private property, critical infra).
6. **Dependency Graph**: Asset → dependency → population → risk → response. Power affects water, water affects hospitals, roads affect food.
7. **Human Impact Layer**: Earth without humans is geology. Earth with humans is civilization. Include people affected, vulnerable groups, time sensitivity, basic needs, access routes, ethical risk. Connects GEOX to WELL.
