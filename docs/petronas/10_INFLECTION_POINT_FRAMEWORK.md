# 10 — Inflection-Point Framework (Leading/Lagging Signals + 3 Decision Rules)

> **Source:** 2026-06-08 strategic analysis (referenced in session) + Acemoglu diagnosis.
> **Status:** Canonical discipline. This is how you time the exit without panic and without lingering.

## The Discipline

> **The decision is not binary, but one of optimal timing. In an era of "simulative" corporate behaviour, neither loyalty nor panic alone suffice — what's needed is disciplined observation and readiness.**

## Leading Signals (early warnings)

Watch for these to decide when to REPOSITION or begin EXIT preparation:

| Signal | What to watch | How to monitor |
|--------|---------------|-----------------|
| **Strategic Shift in Core Business** | Major asset spinoffs (like SEARAH) indicating reduced commitment to certain regions or activities | Press releases, townhalls, business unit closures |
| **Budget & Project Cuts** | Significant drop in exploration or capex budgets (~23% capex cut in 2025); project cancellations/delays (LNG Canada overruns) | Internal budget memos, project status reports |
| **Key Talent Exodus** | Pattern of respected technical leaders or peers leaving — often a sign of diminishing future opportunities | LinkedIn movements, internal networks, MSS uptake |
| **Culture & Engagement Deterioration** | Erosion of merit-based promotions (prolonged freezes, reassignments unrelated to core skills), rising bureaucracy | Townhall tone, internal feedback, peer observations |

## Lagging Indicators (clear signs — too late to act passively)

When these fire, exit is no longer optional:

| Indicator | What it means | Action |
|-----------|---------------|--------|
| **Non-placement or Retrenchment Risk** | Informed that no meaningful role is available after multiple redeployment attempts | Activate Path C (MSS) or D (Retrenchment) immediately |
| **Major Safety or Integrity Lapses** | Repeated incidents undermining personal safety or corporate viability (e.g. FSO Sepat lifeboat fatality) | Exit window confirmed. Don't be the last one out. |
| **Credit Downgrades & Cost of Capital Spikes** | Rating agencies cut credit rating/outlook (Fitch BBB+ affirmed Dec 2025, but any downgrade = severe financial stress) | Acute exit. arifOS becomes the only vessel. |
| **Continued Talent Drain** | Even less-mobile or loyal employees begin leaving = broad loss of confidence | Confirm trajectory. Trigger T11 tripwire. |

## Three Decision Rules

### Rule 1: STAY & CONTRIBUTE

**When:**
- Your role remains core to value creation
- The company shows signs of stabilising (profits bottoming out or rising; rightsizing completed; no critical structural crises pending)
- Rational example: technical upstream function with important projects (basin study, new PSC exploration), profits expected to recover with stable oil prices, PETROS legal challenge on path to settlement

**Action:** Continue adding value. Build arifOS in parallel.

### Rule 2: REPOSITION INTERNALLY

**When:**
- Conditions worsen incrementally
- Leading indicators flash (capex cut, new restructuring)
- You sense your current role could be sidelined
- You need to extend your runway inside PETRONAS

**Action:** Proactive move — seek secondment to a safer team (digital transformation) or a role in a part of the business likely to endure (Production/Operations, New Energy) to extend your runway.

### Rule 3: EXIT (ON YOUR TERMS)

**When:**
- Multiple triggers fire OR one severe lagging indicator hits
- You can ensure financial runway (6-12 months savings or alternative income)
- Maximum benefit timing: leverage MSS or secure a role with at least equal compensation externally
- Example: Sarawak legal battle escalates; exploration portfolio severely cut back; position redefined away from technical work; explicitly designated "surplus"

**Action:** Exit. Ideally via negotiated package or pre-emptive move to a new employer.

## Timing Discipline

> **The goal is to avoid the extremes — neither panic-exit prematurely nor linger until opportunities vanish. Being "right too early" can mean missing out on recovery or severance benefits, whereas leaving too late can mean diminished bargaining power.**

## Personal Stop-Loss Criteria

Define YOUR unacceptable conditions:
- Role move that undermines your technical career
- Large drop in personal learning/growth opportunities
- Cash runway dropping below 3 months
- Sleep debt accumulating past 14 days
- Family stress crossing the line

When these occur → they're your signal to exit. Not a year from now. NOW.

## Watch the Financial Signals

- **If oil prices rebound + profits stabilise:** gauge if benefits flow to reinvestment (inclusive scenario) or are siphoned off in extraordinary dividends (extractive scenario)
- **The latter (persisting extraction despite recovery) = red flag** — suggests short-termism over sustainability, contrary to inclusive principles of reinvesting in talent and growth

## Upstream-Specific Outlooks (YOUR domain)

- **PSC count:** 14 PSCs from MBR 2024 show some commitment. Watch for new PSC rounds in 2025-2026.
- **Exploration budgets:** are they holding? Or being slashed?
- **5-year resource targets:** are they being maintained or quietly walked back?
- **If the exploration pipeline empties** and your skills risk stagnation → inflection point
- **If value-adding exploration projects** (TEA, farm-ins) continue to be supported → company still values those capabilities

## 2027 Reflection Point (canonical)

**Set 2027 as a key reflection point.** By mid-2027 you'll have ~18 months of data post-restructure:

- **If inclusive dynamics returning** (more investment in people, projects, technology, even modest growth): company might be turning a corner
- **If extractive dynamics persist or worsen** (new cost-cut demands, crucial talent leaving, stagnation in Upstream exploration): organisation on a path of decline → execute exit, armed with runway and experience

## The Three Discipline Reminders

1. **Avoid panic-exit.** Stay long enough to harvest the placement, the scarcity premium, and the arifOS runway.
2. **Avoid lingering.** Hard cut-off Mar 2027. The simulive institution's trajectory doesn't wait for sentiment.
3. **Read the signals, not the narratives.** Townhalls perform comfort. Budget memos reveal truth. Talent movements reveal trajectory.
