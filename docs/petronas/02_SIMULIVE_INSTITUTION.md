# 02 — Simulive Institution (Acemoglu Diagnosis)

> **Frame:** Acemoglu & Robinson, *Why Nations Fail* (2012). Nobel Prize 2024.
> **Status:** INTERPRETATION layered on EVIDENCE.

## Core Thesis (Acemoglu)

Nations prosper with **inclusive institutions** (broad participation, property rights, creative destruction, merit-based promotion). Nations fail with **extractive institutions** (elites extract from the rest, suppress innovation, concentrate power).

## PETRONAS — Simulive Institution

PETRONAS is **neither purely inclusive nor purely extractive**. It is a **SIMULATIVE INSTITUTION** — one that has genuine inclusive DNA but where extractive behaviours have captured governance while preserving the inclusive language and rituals.

### Why simulive is more dangerous than pure extractive

(a) Workers believe the inclusive promise → stay too long
(b) Reforms are led by extractors → loop back to extraction
(c) Past achievements provide ALIBI for present extractive behaviour
(d) Creative destruction is suppressed not by force but by bureaucratic friction — innovation dies in PowerPoint

**Soviet parallel:** Acemoglu shows the USSR grew at 6% for decades under extractive institutions — then disintegrated. PETRONAS: RM101.6B (2022) → RM80.7B (2023) → RM55.1B (2024) → RM45.4B (2025) = **–55% in 3 years**. The trajectory is the empirical proof.

**The institution is the shadow. The humans are the substance.** But the shadow moves, and people believe the shadow is alive.

## The CURE is not reform (INTERPRETATION)

You cannot reform a simulive institution from inside. Reforms designed by the same VP/SGM layer that created the bureaucracy loop back to the same pattern. The FORGE→COMFORT→SILO→REFORM cycle has been observed multiple times at PETRONAS:

- **FORGE** (1974-1988): Razaleigh, Rastam, Raja Mohar. Built from nothing. Fought Shell and Exxon. Financial discipline.
- **COMFORT** (1982-1988): "A comfort zone then crept in." Silo mentality, mini-empires.
- **SILO** (late 1980s): Strong characters built fiefdoms.
- **REFORM** (1988-1995): Azizan + Basir dismantled mini-empires. Launched shared values. Globalisation route.
- **FORGE 2.0** (1995-2010): Hassan Marican. Global expansion. Iraq. Majnoon.
- **COMFORT 2.0** (2010-2020): Comfortable giant. High margins. Enabler bloat to 16,000/52,000 (31%).
- **SILO 2.0** (2020-2025): VP bersepah. DSG vendor lock-in. USD16.3M for 2 apps. AI workshops at W Hotel. Cloud-washing. PETROS rising.
- **REFORM 2.0?** (2025-2026): Rightsizing cuts 5,000 enablers — but same CEO, same VPs, same vendor contracts, same culture.

**The question mark:** who is breaking the current comfort? Not the rightsizing — it cuts enablers, not the VP layer. Not the SEARAH JV — it dumps mature assets, not the bureaucracy. **REFORM 2.0 is COMFORT performing as REFORM.**

## Universe 25 Mapping (Calhoun, 1968)

Calhoun's Universe 25 died not from resource scarcity but from **behavioural sink** — when the environment is comfortable enough that survival no longer requires effort, the colony stops reproducing meaningful behaviour and descends into grooming, withdrawal, and eventual extinction.

| Universe 25 | PETRONAS 2026 |
|-------------|--------------|
| Resources abundant | RM217B cash mid-2024. Among world's most profitable NOCs |
| No external threat | PETROS is the only real threat — and it's being litigated, not fought |
| Beautiful Ones emerge | VPs who manage managers who manage managers. AI workshops at W Hotel. Cloud-washing accepted without question |
| Reproduction stops | No new discoveries of PETRONAS DNA quality. The orphan employees who care are being "rightsized" or ignored |
| Grooming replaces function | Townhalls replace action. Scorecards replace thinking. "Digital transformation" KPIs replace actual transformation |
| The colony doesn't know it's dying | "If we don't do it now, there will be no PETRONAS in 10 years" — CEO said this, but the reform doesn't touch the actual disease |

**The disease is not enabler bloat. The disease is the gap between the founding DNA and the current culture. You can cut 5,000 enablers and still have a company full of Beautiful Ones — because the Beautiful Ones are not in the enabler layer. They're in the VP layer. Rastam would have cut the VPs, not the office boys.**
