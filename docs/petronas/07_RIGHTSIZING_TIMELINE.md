## Why So Slow? (INTERPRETATION)

1. **Legal compliance** — Malaysian Employment Act requires structured notification, consultation with Department of Labour and Department of Industrial Relations. You can't fire 5,000 people overnight.
2. **Placement process** — They're not just cutting. They redesign the org → map people to new roles → those who don't fit get MSS → those who reject MSS face retrenchment. That's a multi-stakeholder pipeline: supervisors, HRBPs, Transition Council, PDCs.
3. **The CEO's survival calculation** — see hantu map (file 02).

## The 5,200 Roles Elimination

- **Total target:** ~5,200 roles (predominantly corporate & support units)
- **Cost saving:** ~RM1B/year (~2% of 2025 opex)
- **Concentrated in:** enablers (admin, support, corporate)
- **Not touching:** VP/SGM/GM layer. The 5-layer bureaucracy survives intact.

## The Inverse Selection Effect (INTERPRETATION, supported by evidence)

As the 18-month process drags on, **the most employable leave first** (via voluntary MSS, external offers, or VSS). The less marketable stay. This is the "inverse selection" — the institution's quality declines by attrition, not by direct cuts.

**For you:** the inverse selection inverts. You have the option (arifOS) so you don't HAVE to leave. The vessel IS the protection. See file 06.
