# 009: THE EARTH ORGAN CONTRACT

> **Epistemic Status:** SOVEREIGN ARCHITECTURE
> **Scope:** Defines the scope and ontology of GEOX as the Physical Earth Memory and Reasoning Organ within the arifOS Federation.

## 1. The Organ's Scope
GEOX is not a seismic app or a toolbox; it is the **Enterprise Subsurface Co-Processor**. It owns physical-reality claims across well correlation, seismic interpretation, structural uncertainty, stratigraphy, inversion, reservoir framing, and 4D update loops.

GEOX emits governed claim objects rather than raw data grids or prose. Every claim (horizon, fault, velocity, 4D delta) carries provenance, evidence handles, a validity state, and a usage policy.

## 2. The Earth Ontology
To prevent semantic drift, GEOX is built on a strict layered ontology:
1. **Observation:** Raw logs, checkshots, fossils, core, pressure tests.
2. **Interpretation:** Tops, faults, facies, closures, contacts.
3. **Model:** Velocity cubes, static grids, dynamic states, petro-elastic priors.
4. **Decision Constraint:** Drilling windows, trap confidence, volumetric trust, "advisory only" flags.

## 3. The Federated Transport Doctrine
Heavyweight binary Earth volumes do NOT travel through the MCP JSON transport. 
GEOX transmits **Claim Packets** that include a verdict and a manifest reference. 
The transport pattern is:
`session_init → identity bind → intent route → GEOX claim generation → floor/risk/judge checks → signed render/decision payload → selective downstream fetch`.

GEOX packets also carry domain-specific semantics: depth domain, datum, CRS, velocity-model version, stratigraphic frame, well basis, survey vintage, and interpolation state.

## 4. The Six Bands of Operation
GEOX exposes its MCP tools across six functional bands:
- `observe` (ingest logs/seismic/well markers)
- `correlate` (well tie, strat tie, biostrat tie)
- `infer` (faults, structure, facies, contacts, properties)
- `simulate` (rock-physics links, volumetrics, scenario propagation)
- `audit` (uncertainty, QC, lineage, contradictions)
- `render` (thin payloads plus cube manifests for visualization)

**GEOX never returns naked certainty.** Every Earth claim is accompanied by its physics basis, uncertainty, conflict register, and allowed-use contract.
