# 007: THE GEOX CHARTER — VELOCITY IS NOT GEOLOGY

> **Epistemic Status:** SOVEREIGN CHARTER
> **Scope:** Defines the unbreakable loop between GEOX (Geophysical Intelligence) and Geological Earth Intelligence within the arifOS Federation.

## The Core Axiom: Velocity is a Symptom, not the Earth
A seismic velocity model is a mathematical construct optimized to flatten image gathers and minimize traveltime residuals. It is one of many possible models that fit the data. **Geology**—stratigraphy, burial history, overpressure, and tectonics—is the actual Earth. If GEOX runs on velocity alone, it is generating geophysical symptoms, not geological truth. The machinery forged in this repository exists to enforce that distinction.

## 1. Uncertainty Lives Between the Disciplines
Geophysical measurements inherently carry ambiguity that expands rapidly away from well control. Geological intelligence (regional compaction trends, structural style, analogs) is the only force capable of breaking that degeneracy. GEOX computes the Anomalous Contrast (AC) risk and the uncertainty band; Geology makes the billion-dollar decision on whether to risk the trap. It is a loop, not a one-way pipe.

## 2. Real-Time is Geological, Not Just Kinematic
When a new checkshot or Logging-While-Drilling (LWD) measurement arrives, GEOX updates the velocity proxy and emits a real-time risk state. But real-time data is only valuable when contextualized. Geological intelligence translates an "AC Risk 0.74, Anisotropy failed" verdict into an operational decision: *Do we drill the flank, sidetrack, or stand down?* Without the geological loop, real-time geophysics provides fast answers to the wrong questions.

## 3. The Loop Protects Against Overfitting
Advanced algorithms (FWI, Tomography, Machine Learning) will always push velocity models to fit the seismic data more tightly. Geological intelligence provides the essential pushback when a "better mathematical fit" violates physical rock properties or basin evolution. GEOX explores the model space; Geology dictates which parts of that space are allowed to drive decisions.

## 4. Provenance is a Social Contract
Every velocity proxy emitted by GEOX carries an explicit lineage and a list of evidence handles pointing to QC artifacts. This is not just metadata—it is a socio-technical contract. The Federation Usage Policy physically blocks autonomous agents from blindly drilling or estimating volumes on unvalidated proxies. The loop ensures there is always a human Sovereign (F13) and a geological context ready to audit the evidence and exercise the override.
