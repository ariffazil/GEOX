## Claim
After Proto–South China Sea subduction terminated and Dangerous Grounds underthrusting built the
inherited architecture, **extension propagated across north and central Sabah** (Crocker–Kinabalu–Telupid
sector), overprinting the collisional architecture. Its crustal/lithospheric expression contributes to
thinning, lower-crustal melting, uplift/exhumation (Kinabalu granite 7.85–7.22 Ma) and structural
reactivation. The extension **may** be dynamically linked to rollback, slab-remnant evolution,
delamination, or a lithospheric drip.

1. **Crustal thinning pattern** — β distribution across north-central Sabah, Moho shallowing beneath the
   corridor. Test: RF transect + VDSS.
2. **Extensional fault kinematics** — normal/oblique-sense faults with post-collision timing. Test: structural mapping, slip analysis, growth strata.
3. **Timing** — extensional fabrics, veins, dykes; exhumation; granite 7.85–7.22 Ma as anchor. Test: U–Pb/Ar–Ar, thermochronology.
4. **Receiver-function Moho variation** — corridor vs flanks. Test: RF line along A–A′.
5. **Seismic anisotropy** — corridor-parallel asthenospheric flow (rollback) vs steep fabric (drip). Test: shear-wave splitting.
6. **Gravity/magnetic trends** — broad thinning (continental) vs narrow dense strip (oceanic). Test: Bouguer+FTG with uncertainty; magnetics AS/RTE (low-latitude caveats apply).
7. **Basin subsidence + thermal history** — syn-extension subsidence, Ro/Tmax anomalies. Test: backstrip + wells.
8. **Melting evidence** — lower-crustal melts (M-PILIA23 consequence), any dated intrusions along the axis (ties to G-A tests).

## Falsifiers (H-EXT-01 dies if…)
1. **No spatially coherent thinning** — β flat or scattered across the sector (kills the "corridor" specifically).
2. **Kinematics purely contractional/transpressional** in the post-~23 Ma interval (no extension anywhere).
3. **No post-subduction extension timing** — all extensional fabrics pre-date subduction termination.
4. **Incompatible geochronology** — exhumation/melting ages not overlapping any plausible extension window.
5. **A discrete alternative fits all observations** while H-EXT-01 fails (e.g. purely gravity-driven with zero deep signature).
6. **Oceanic crust proven** — would not falsify extension, but would falsify the *continental* scope as stated → fork to H-EXT-02 (oceanic variant) rather than mutate this ID.
