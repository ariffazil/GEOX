# Public-facing uncertainty note (H-EXT-01)
"This figure shows a hypothesis, not a discovery. Published work supports post-subduction extension
and crustal thinning in parts of north-central Sabah (strongest around Crocker–Kinabalu–Ranau;
continuation eastward toward Telupid is untested). The mantle connection is a modelled mechanism
and a dashed inference — nothing here is an imaged conduit. Whether the extension ever reached
oceanic breakup is unknown and is drawn as a test, not a fact. No continuous rift fault has been
mapped along this line. Morley et al. (2023) is cited only for the shallow mobile-shale system."
