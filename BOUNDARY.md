GEOX is the Earth evidence organ. It prepares, computes, and constrains
geoscience claims through physical evidence. It does not decide policy, capital
allocation, or constitutional verdicts.

## Owns

- Earth evidence ingestion, QC, and artifact emission.
- Well logs, petrophysics, stratigraphy, seismic, maps, basin charge,
  volumetrics, prospect risk, and physical-feasibility checks.
- Physics-9 and domain-specific hold/void conditions for Earth claims.
- GEOX MCP tools, schemas, examples, and geological output envelopes.
- Traceable evidence references for geological interpretation and prospect
  evaluation.

## Does Not Own

- arifOS constitutional law, `888_JUDGE`, `999_SEAL`, or VAULT999 authority.
- WEALTH capital allocation, investment decisions, or portfolio commitments.
- AAA cockpit UX or A2A identity ownership.
- A-FORGE deployment substrate or production rollout authority.
- Human final judgment over geological or business claims.

## Imports From

- arifOS: governance envelope rules, claim-state vocabulary, floor policy, and
  federation routing.
- A-FORGE: deployment/runtime environment and service health substrate.
- AAA: operator/session context for displayed GEOX work.
- WEALTH: economic scenario references when field-development constraints are
  needed.
- Data sources: LAS/well files, seismic volumes, maps, basin ontologies, and
  validated user-supplied evidence.

## Exports To

- arifOS: governed Earth evidence envelopes, contradiction scans, prospect risk,
  and artifact references.
- WEALTH: physically constrained prospect inputs, reserves/risk ranges, and
  development assumptions.
- AAA: operator-visible geological reports, cards, maps, and health summaries.
- A-FORGE: runtime health, image/package expectations, and deploy probes.
