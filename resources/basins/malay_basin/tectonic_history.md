# Malay Basin Tectonic History

The Malay Basin is an elongate Cenozoic basin (~500 km long, ~200 km wide) trending northwest-southeast in the southern South China Sea. 

## Key Evolutionary Phases:
1. **Late Eocene to Oligocene (Syn-rift Phase):** Extensional rift basin formation. Deposition of continental, lacustrine sediment (Group M, L).
2. **Early Miocene (Transition Phase):** Sag phase, deposition of Group K, J, and I fluvial-deltaic systems.
3. **Middle to Late Miocene (Inversion Phase):** Compressive tectonic inversion, creating anticlines and structural traps (Group H, F, E, D).
4. **Pliocene to Present (Post-inversion Phase):** Regional subsidence, marine transgression (Group A, B, C).
