# Kinabalu Basin — upper-plate clock

Position: UPPER PLATE of the NW Borneo Collision System. Basin fill resting on
the Borneo (Crocker) accretionary wedge and its seaward flank — a group of
wedge-top and foreland depocentres, not a single-origin basin.

## Clock (this position's expression)
- Before ~23 Ma: not a basin in the modern sense. Its SUBSTRATE is the
  actively accreting Crocker prism — proto-South China Sea material subducted/
  accreted beneath NW Borneo (polarity + timing: HOLD, see
  northwest_borneo_trough). Crocker/Temburong/Setap-type deep-marine sediment
  varies by province; Stage II–III terminology is problematic here.
- BASE_MIOCENE_STYLE_CHANGE (~23 Ma): wedge style shifts accretion →
  underthrusting when thinned CONTINENTAL crust starts entering the trench
  zone. BMU/Top-Crocker sits near base Miocene onshore-north — it is NOT a
  synonym of the DRU.
- COLLISION_MM (debated ~20.5 vs 16.9–15.5 Ma): thrust loading, uplift and
  erosion — the block goes UP while the lower plate goes DOWN.
- POST_COLLISION_UPLIFT_DRU (13.5–12.5 Ma): the Deep Regional Unconformity —
  major erosion, then prograding deltas: **Stage IV begins**. This is the
  most traceable surface in the province.
- WEDGE_FRONT_ADVANCE_UIU (13–10.5 Ma): mini-basins, mud volcanoes,
  Rotan-type turbidite fairways ponded by shale topography.
- SRU_LATE_MIOCENE (~9–8.5 Ma): inboard belt folded + eroded; outboard belt
  and East Baram depocentres open — the basin walks outward.
- Pliocene–Recent: gentle anticlines and crestal faults outboard; IVG
  progradation to NN19/NN18 family.

## Stage IV calibration (2026 enterprise NN standard — PRELIMINARY)
IVG~NN19 · IVF~NN18 · IVE~NN16 (intra-IVE: E3/NN15, E2/NN11B, E1/NN11A) ·
IVD~top NN10 · IVC~top NN9 · IVB~NN8 · IVA~mid-NN5 · Stage III top = HOLD.
Detailed wells show overlap, reworking and long zonal ranges: NN zone ≠
surface. Surfaces require seismic-stratal criterion + age bracket.

## Why this province is the correlation MASTER
Continuous progradational archive = best time resolution in the system.
Correlate OUTWARD from here. Never transfer Stage IV boxes into
Layang-Layang (carbonate-and-drift package, different origin) or read them
across the mobile-shale moat without physical ties.

## What it records
CONTINUOUS TIME (upper plate), punctuated by the DRU erosion event.
At depth, part of this basin may sit stacked on underthrust Dangerous Grounds
crust — INTERPRET, untested; the falsifier is a well-tied dip line across the
trough.
