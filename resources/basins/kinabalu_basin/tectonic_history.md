# Kinabalu Basin — upper-plate clock

Position: UPPER PLATE of the NW Borneo Collision System. Basin fill resting on
the Borneo (Crocker) accretionary wedge and its seaward flank — a group of
wedge-top and foreland depocentres, not a single-origin basin.

## Clock (this position's expression)
- Before ~23 Ma: not a basin in the modern sense. Its SUBSTRATE is the
  actively accreting Crocker prism — proto-South China Sea material subducted/
  accreted beneath NW Borneo (polarity + timing: HOLD, see
  northwest_borneo_trough). Crocker/Temburong/Setap-type deep-marine sediment
  varies by province; Stage II–III terminology is problematic here.
- BASE_MIOCENE_STYLE_CHANGE (~23 Ma): wedge style shifts accretion →
  underthrusting when thinned CONTINENTAL crust starts entering the trench
  zone. BMU/Top-Crocker sits near base Miocene onshore-north — it is NOT a
  synonym of the DRU.
- COLLISION_MM (debated ~20.5 vs 16.9–15.5 Ma): thrust loading, uplift and
  erosion — the block goes UP while the lower plate goes DOWN.
- POST_COLLISION_UPLIFT_DRU (13.5–12.5 Ma): the Deep Regional Unconformity —
  major erosion, then prograding deltas: **Stage IV begins**. This is the
  most traceable surface in the province.
- WEDGE_FRONT_ADVANCE_UIU (13–10.5 Ma): mini-basins, mud volcanoes,
  Rotan-type turbidite fairways ponded by shale topography.
- SRU_LATE_MIOCENE (~9–8.5 Ma, AGE HOLD): the Shallow Regional Unconformity —
  inboard belt folded + eroded WHILE transtension opened the outboard belt and
  East Baram depocentres; the basin walks outward. Full settlement below (§ SRU).
- Pliocene–Recent: gentle anticlines and crestal faults outboard; IVG
  progradation to NN19/NN18 family.

## The SRU (Shallow Regional Unconformity) — settlement, sovereign 2026-10-01
**Identity.** Named by Levell (1987) with the DRU, the LIU/UIU and Horizons
III–I, and given biostratigraphic ages. Process: strong compression of the
**Inboard Belt** — folding, uplift, erosion — at the same time as **transtension
at the western margin**, which opened the **Outboard Belt** and the East Baram
depocentres. This switch set up the Late Miocene sediment-routing system that
charged the IVD–IVG reservoirs. The SRU is the Kinabalu wedge rearranging itself.

**Kinabalu only.** The SRU is an upper-plate surface. It is NOT one of the
invariants — no single unconformity reflector runs through all three provinces.
What is invariant is the order of events and the arrangement of surfaces around
the collision; the one surface family reaching both plates is LANGGAR, and every
other unconformity sits either BELOW it on the lower plate or ABOVE it on the
upper plate.

**Age and stage — HOLD.** ~9–8.5 Ma in the literature; internal values conflict:
9.53 Ma at top NN9, ~8.6 Ma at base NN10A, 8.50 Ma in the Rotan PDA. Stage
position unresolved: top IVC in some files, base IVD in the Atlas. ASAS–MALAM:
AM-09 SENJA. Age is HOLD until the 888 decision on Rotan-1.

**What to map.** The two hinge lines: where the SRU erosion stops and where its
onlap stops along KL1–KL5. Those two lines give the first map of the Late
Miocene uplift. Reading rules: angular discordance = tilt; hiatus-triangle width
(Krebs) = depth of erosion, i.e. a contour map of uplift; erosion→conformity
transition = hinge, separating source from sink.

**Why it matters.** Reservoir: IVD–IVE fairways incl. the Rotan pay (NN10) depend
on SRU uplift supplying sediment. Trap: SRU deformation timing vs diapir growth
controls whether Rotan-type closure against the diapir pre-dates charge. Seal:
inboard SRU erosion can breach seals — offshore, where the SRU is conformable,
it cannot. Correlation: treating the inboard angular SRU and a deepwater zone
boundary as one thing is the Rotan "IVC vs IVD" error — how an age model goes
wrong.
