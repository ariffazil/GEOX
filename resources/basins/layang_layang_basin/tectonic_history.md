# Layang-Layang Basin — lower-plate clock

Position: LOWER PLATE of the NW Borneo Collision System. Continental crust
rifted off the South China conjugate margin, drifted south on the South China
Sea plate, drowned in situ when collision jammed the trench to the SE.

## Clock (one system, this position's expression)
- RIFT_ONSET_PALEOCENE → ROU (top pre-rift/base syn-rift). Half-graben mosaic;
  age approximate, NOT a basinwide datum.
- RIFT_PHASE2_EOCENE → IRU (early/late syn-rift separator). Sporadic; merges
  with BU in places.
- BREAKUP_EARLY_OLIGOCENE → BU. Diachronous process surface. Uplift and
  erosion of rift shoulders, then thermal sag. On inherited highs, carbonate
  growth may start BEFORE the time-equivalent BU — BU ≠ base carbonate.
- DRIFT_CARBONATE_CHATTIAN_AQUITANIAN → carbonate interval. Platform and reef
  carbonates on the forebulge shallow zone (Steuer et al.); the main reservoir
  window. Carbonate top is an environmental/age marker — NOT strictly an
  unconformity — and is often more consistently mappable than the tectonic
  surfaces.
- COLLISION_MM → RU. Carbonate demise and drowning; pelagic (red) drape; NO
  major erosion at the surface itself; the block goes DOWN. Spreading in the
  SCS died at about the same time (trench jam, Clift et al. 2008). RU is
  uncertain toward the Sabah Trough and is NOT necessarily the same reflector
  as the shelf DRU.
- POST_COLLISION → sediment-starved drape. PLIOCENE → stable.

## What it records
MISSING TIME. This province is an unconformity archive: its value is in
process ORDER (ROU → IRU → BU → carbonate → RU), which is more reliable than
the absolute age of any one surface. Treat the sequence as a relative tectonic
order, not three synchronous chronohorizons.

## Open questions (HOLD)
- Collision age: ~20.5 Ma vs ~16.9–15.5 Ma brackets.
- Syn-rift lithology: no well penetrates the complete package; deep
  lithological interpretation "highly uncertain" (2024 DG framework).
- Atoll subsidence vs "no drowned reefs" claim (Hutchison 2010) — contradiction
  preserved, unarbitrated.

## Charge problem (enterprise)
Reservoir presence rides on carbonate highs; the real risk is CHARGE from a
syn-rift source — untested. Do not import Kinabalu charge assumptions here.
