# Sabah Basin Tectonic History

## Overview
The Sabah Basin is located in northern Sabah, East Malaysia, on the NW Borneo collision margin: Paleogene Proto-South China Sea (PSCS) subduction beneath northern Borneo terminated with the Early Miocene collision and underthrusting of Dangerous Grounds/Reed Bank continental crust (Hall 2013). Present-day Sundaland–Philippine Sea Plate convergence is at most a far-field contributor to modern deformation — it did not build NW Sabah. [Plate framing corrected 2026-10-13 per external audit relay (Arif); previous text said "Eurasian vs Philippine Sea Plate collision zone".] The basin evolved through five major tectonic phases from the Late Eocene to the present.

**CRITICAL UPDATE (NSPW synthesis 2026-07-16):** The basin is NOT a simple passive-margin fold belt. It is dominated by the North Sabah–Pagasa Wedge (NSPW), a >500 km syn-collisional, mobile-shale-dominated, sediment-loaded wedge formed after Dangerous Grounds continental crust underthrust NW Borneo. The NSPW is the mechanical spine connecting deep tectonic forcing to shallow stratigraphy and petroleum systems.

## Phase 1: Syn-Rift / Platform Setup (Paleogene–Early Miocene, 40–23 Ma) — ABKSS: ASAS + BEBAS
- PSCS rifting; Dangerous Grounds rift blocks
- Deep marine turbiditic deposition of the Panas Thicknessnam Formation
- Nido Limestone growing on Dangerous Grounds platform
- Source rock potential: TOC 0.5–3.0%, Type II kerogen
- BEBAS (Oligocene intra-rift reorganisation) = Arif's independent contribution

## Phase 2: Collision Onset (~23 Ma) — ABKSS: KAPUR — BMU/TCU
- Dangerous Grounds continental crust enters subduction zone
- Cannot subduct (buoyant) → underthrusts NW Borneo
- End of Crocker accretionary prism era
- Beginning of NSPW as post-collisional syn-orogenic wedge
- **This is NOT the DRU.** BMU/TCU marks collision onset; DRU marks wedge-top.
- Note (audit 2026-10-13): ~23–24 Ma is the STRATIGRAPHIC unconformity age (TCU/BMU, Lunt 2022 re-dates to c. 24 Ma); the GEODYNAMIC end of PSCS subduction is separately estimated at ~21 Ma (Gilligan et al. receiver functions). Keep surface-age and process-timing as two linked entities, never one number.

## Phase 3: Wedge Growth (23–13 Ma) — NSPW Active Phase
- Massive sediment flux from Borneo highlands → NW Sabah margin
- NSPW builds to 8–9 km thick pile
- Imbrication, folding, mass transport, sediment incorporation
- Overpressures develop in Miocene shales
- Mini-basins / pocket basins form (2–15 km wide, 3–4 km deep)
- Shale expulsion begins

## Phase 4: Slab Detachment + Shale Expulsion (~13–10.5 Ma) — ABKSS: SABAR — DRU
- PSCS slab detachment — mechanism OPEN (break-off vs lithospheric drip vs delamination vs crustal melting): slab remnant >250 km imaged by Pilia, Rawlinson et al. 2023 (Gondwana Research) arrival-time tomography; drip-driven post-subduction extension modelled by Pilia et al. 2023 (Nature Geoscience). Do not state "slab breaks off" as settled fact.
- Buoyant rebound uplifts NW Borneo hinterland (Crocker Range: 24–60 km crust)
- DRU carved into shelf — NOT collision onset, it is wedge-top
- Massive delta systems begin prograding
- Shale-expulsion basins develop above overpressured mobile shale
- **DRU reclassification:** from "collision unconformity" to "wedge-top / slab-detachment response" (attribution = SYNTHESIS/INT hypothesis, per ontology tag 2026-07)
- DRU age: 13.5–12.5 Ma (Morley et al. 2023); ~12–13 Ma (Lunt 2022). Treat DRU as a diachronous FAMILY (DRU-shelf / DRU-Spine / DRU-TB / DRU-onshore-TCU-composite), each member with its own marker + calibration tag; the ~15.5 Ma spreading-end tie is a plate-event hypothesis, not a dated marker.

## Phase 5: Mud Canopy + Post-Wedge Burial (10.5–0 Ma) — ABKSS: SENJA
- Overpressured shales expelled through pipes and vents
- 50 mud volcano centres coalesce into 1900 km² canopy
- Late middle-to-early late Miocene turbidites deposited around canopies
- Rotan field turbidite sandstones deposited in this window
- SRU (AGE HOLD ~9–8.5 Ma; candidates: 9.53 Ma top NN9-hamatus / ~8.6 Ma base NN10A; three inboard wells validate ~9.36–9.41 Ma and reject 8.5–8.6 inboard — see kinabalu_basin claims): post-canopy burial by prograding shelf/delta systems
- Late gravity-driven DWFTB develops
- Present-day mud volcanoes still active

## Key Unconformities (NSPW-Reclassified)
- BMU/TCU (23–24 Ma) — collision onset / NSPW base (surface age; subduction end ~21 Ma is a separate process timing)
- DRU (13.5–12.5 Ma) — wedge-top / slab-detachment response (NOT collision start); diachronous family
- UIU (10.5 Ma) — end of mini-basin development
- SRU (~9–8.5 Ma, AGE HOLD) — post-canopy burial; candidates conflict, never quote a single number without its marker
