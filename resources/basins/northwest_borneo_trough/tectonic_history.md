# Northwest Borneo Trough — the trench that died of indigestion

Position: the Fossil TRENCH AXIS of the NW Borneo Collision System — the plate
boundary whose jamming created the other three positions' modern geometry.
Recorded as an entity so the unresolved polarity/timing controversy is
queryable rather than laundered into a chart footnote.

## Two live models (HOLD — do not average)
1. OLDER (Hamilton 1979 lineage): SE-directed subduction of proto–South China
   Sea material beneath the NW Borneo embayment; active trench during SCS
   spreading (C12 onward ~33–32 Ma); "widely accepted as a now extinct plate
   margin". Kinabalu/Brunei embayment = rollback product; Crocker = accreted
   prism.
2. NEWER (mobile-shale + 2023–2025 DG enterprise work): emphasis shifts to
   UNDERTHRUSTING of thinned CONTINENTAL crust from ~base Miocene (~23 Ma wedge
   style change). Collision effects propagate earlier and deeper than the classic
   Middle-Miocene single-event picture.

The two models disagree on POLARITY and on WHEN the main consumption stopped —
which changes what BU, DRU and RU each 'should' look like at depth. Neither is
disproven. Verdict: HOLD. New evidence required: the well-tied dip line across
the trough, plus provenance flips across the axis.

## Death of the trench
COLLISION_MM: the Dangerous Grounds arrives; trench jams; SCS spreading dies
(~16.9–15.5 Ma per Clift et al. 2008; ~20.5 Ma alternative retained).
Followed by inversion/rebound along the axis; the modern Sabah Trough sits on a
modified expression of this line as the system's foredeep.
