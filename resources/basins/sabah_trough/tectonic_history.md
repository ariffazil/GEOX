# Sabah Trough — the flexural moat

Position: FOREDEEP of the NW Borneo Collision System — the place where the
lower plate bends down beneath the load of the advancing wedge. It is a
bathymetric and flexural feature, not a basin with its own origin; its fill is
the distal archive of what happened on either side of it.

## Clock (this position's expression)
- Rift: inherits the same South China conjugate margin system; not yet a trough.
- Breakup (BREAKUP_EARLY_OLIGOCENE): post-rift sag; thin/reworked carbonates
  during DRIFT_CARBONATE_CHATTIAN_AQUITANIAN.
- COLLISION_MM: foredeep onset — maximum flexure with forebulge outboard on the
  platform. THE ONSET IS HARD TO TRACE: what is an erosional surface on the
  wedge side becomes onlap, condensed interval or subtle facies change here.
- POST_COLLISION_UPLIFT_DRU: receives distal gravity flows shed off the
  uplifting Borneo wedge.
- WEDGE_FRONT_ADVANCE_UIU (13–10.5 Ma): the wedge front overrides the trough
  margin — mini-basins, mud chambers/pipes, volcanic mud flows and canopy
  complexes. Wedge-top derived chaos blankets the moat.
- SRU_LATE_MIOCENE (~9–8.5 Ma): deep, low-energy fill.
- Pliocene–Recent: stable to mildly deformed; folding concentrated at margins.

## The quiet-floor paradox
Flat bathymetry ≠ quiet subsurface. Mobile shale disrupts the temporal record:
it remobilizes older sediment, creates local unconformities, alters
accommodation, deflects turbidite pathways and produces similar-looking
seismic terminations at DIFFERENT times. A single coloured line on a regional
chart implies one instantaneous event; the well record shows a time-gap field.

## What it records
DISTURBED TIME. Correlating through this position requires physical surface +
stratal geometry + age bracket together. Fossil-zone equivalence alone is
invalid across the trough.
