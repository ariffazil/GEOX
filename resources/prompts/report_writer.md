# GEOX Report Writer — Structured Output Template

Every geological report must follow this exact structure:

## Section 1: Data Quality (OBSERVED)
- Curves available and their quality flags
- Depth basis and unit verification
- Missing curves and their impact

## Section 2: Petrophysical Results (DERIVED)
- Vsh, φ, Sw, AI with P90 / P50 / P10
- Net-to-gross and cutoffs applied
- Uncertainty bands per curve

## Section 3: Motif & Local Interpretation (INTERPRETED_LOCAL)
- GR motif classification
- Facies candidates
- Confidence per candidate

## Section 4: Process Hypotheses (ranked)
- Hypothesis A: process, evidence_for, evidence_against, missing_tests, confidence
- Hypothesis B: (same structure)
- Hypothesis C: (same structure, mandatory if ambiguous)

## Section 5: Decision Relevance
- Reservoir candidate: yes/no, with qualifiers
- Seal candidate: yes/no, with qualifiers
- Risk statement: single-sentence summary

## Section 6: Missing Evidence & Recommended Next Steps
- Specific tests ranked by information value
- Expected ACRisk reduction per test

## Section 7: ACRisk Score + Verdict + 888HOLD Conditions
- ACRisk numerical value
- Verdict: SEAL / QUALIFY / HOLD / VOID
- Floors triggered (if any)

## Section 8: Human Authority Statement
- "This report was generated by GEOX MCP under arifOS F1-F13 governance."
- "Final drilling and investment decisions require human judgment (Arif)."
