Earth Witness

A photo is not the rock. This page writes down what the picture shows, asks for the tests a camera cannot do, and stops at a candidate. It does not seal a well, a prospect, or an age.

Reading organ status…

Public and synthetic pictures only. Do not upload confidential or operational data. Location tags are stripped, the file is hashed, and nothing is kept after the reply. Tanya Bumi — tanya gambar, bukan simpan data.

Put a ruler in the frame when you can. A coin or a hand is only a rough size. Compton’s field rule still holds: measure, then name.

Scale in the frame

A measured ruler is calibrated. A coin (~24 mm) or a hand (~180 mm) is a hint, and the reply will still ask for a real scale.

Display, not the earth

A bright spot is not gas. Amplitude is not a fluid. Say the display polarity and whether the axis is time or depth. Without polarity, a hard kick and a soft kick cannot be told apart.

Shape is not an age

The picture can support a look-alike. An age needs a taxon and a public range (Paleobiology Database), then a person. Cuttings can be caved from higher up the hole.

What this page will not do
  • Call oil or gas from a bright spot.
  • Date a fossil from its shape.
  • Treat a basin name as evidence.
  • Go past a qualified candidate. A seal, if one is ever made, is a separate act.
