# Morley 2023 — Collisional Wedge

## Key Claims

1. NW Sabah deformation is driven by collisional wedge mechanics (NW-SE compression).
2. Mobile shales are a passive response to tectonic shortening.
3. Piggy-back basins form above the advancing wedge tip.

## Challenges

- Alternative: Gravity-driven deformation above overpressured shales may dominate.
- Timing: Wedge model requires specific Pliocene-Recent compression rates.
- Seismic evidence: Basal detachment geometry is ambiguous on available 2D data.
