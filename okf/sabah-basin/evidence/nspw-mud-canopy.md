# NSPW Mud Canopy

## Key Findings

- Mud canopy forms a regionally extensive seal above deep-water reservoir fairways.
- Diapiric shale bodies create both structural traps and potential migration barriers.
- Overpressure regime critical for seal integrity assessment.

## Implications

- Prospect risk: HIGH for top-seal integrity in areas of active diapirism.
- Mitigation: AVO and CSEM for hydrocarbon indicator validation below canopy.
