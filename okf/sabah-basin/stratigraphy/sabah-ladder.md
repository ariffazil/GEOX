# Sabah Stratigraphic Framework

## Stages

| Stage | Age | Lithology | Key Surfaces |
|-------|-----|-----------|-------------|
| VI | Pliocene-Pleistocene | Shallow marine clastics | Regional unconformity |
| V | Late Miocene | Wedge-top clastics | Syn-kinematic |
| IVC | Middle Miocene | Turbidite sands | **Primary reservoir** |
| IVB | Middle Miocene | Turbidite sands/shales | Secondary reservoir |
| IVA | Early Miocene | Turbidite sands | Secondary reservoir |
| III | Oligocene-Early Miocene | Deep-water shales | Regional seal |
| II | Eocene | Carbonate platform | Basement? |
| I | Cretaceous-Paleocene | Deep-water shales | Acoustic basement |

## Key Surfaces

- **Base Level IV**: Onset of turbidite deposition
- **Intra-Level IV**: Progradational stacking pattern
- **Base Level VI**: Regional uplift and erosion
