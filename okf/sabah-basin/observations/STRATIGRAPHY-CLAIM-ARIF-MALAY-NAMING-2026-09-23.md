  petroleum system).

  root, BM Penang flavor).

## 5. RIVALS & FALSIFICATION TESTS

**Rival:** Morley 2023 collisional wedge model might explain all
observations through different mechanism (out-of-sequence thrusting,
inversion tectonics).

**Falsification test:** Kalau well-biostratigraphy tie semua
sequence boundaries ke regionally-recognised collision event (post-
Middle Miocene), then "rift extension" interpretation is rejected.
Kalau ada well logs yang show anomalous depth/porosity patterns
that pure collision tak boleh explain, then "rift extension" is
strengthened.

**Counter-evidence:** Tiada wells dengan proven pre-collision
extensional phase from Sabah basin published dalam peer-reviewed
literature yang saya nampak.
