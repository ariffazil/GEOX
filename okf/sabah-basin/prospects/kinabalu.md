# Kinabalu Prospect

## Trap

Four-way dip closure against regional shale weld. Stratigraphic component from onlap onto diapir flanks.

## Reservoir

Deep-water turbidite sands of Stage IVA/IVC. Net pay ~50m from AVO anomaly.

## Risk Factors

- Charge timing relative to trap formation (late Miocene)
- Seal integrity in mobile shale system
- Reservoir presence in distal turbidite lobe
