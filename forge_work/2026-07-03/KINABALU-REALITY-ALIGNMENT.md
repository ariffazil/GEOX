```
40-15 Ma:  Proto-SCS oceanic subduction southward beneath Sabah
           → Crocker accretionary prism built
           → Crust thickened to ~30 km

~20 Ma:    Dangerous Grounds extended continental crust arrives
           → Collision + UNDER THRUSTING (not subduction)
           → Early Miocene uplift, Top Crocker Unconformity
           → Proto-SCS subduction terminates

15-9 Ma:   Celebes Sea subduction northward beneath Sulu Arc
           → Rollback to SE at 17-14 Ma and 13-9 Ma
           → Extension in northern Borneo
           → Semporna arc volcanism (SE Sabah)

~9 Ma:     Subduction ceases beneath Sabah
           → Post-subduction mantle reorganization
           → Possible lithospheric drip or slab break-off

8-7 Ma:    Kinabalu granite emplaced (7.85-7.22 Ma)
           → Fractional crystallization + crustal assimilation
           → Intraplate magma, not arc magma

7-0 Ma:    Rapid exhumation >7 mm/yr
           → Isostatic rebound + rollback extension + mantle processes
           → Summit at 4,095 m
```
