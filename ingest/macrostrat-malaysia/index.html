Geological Map of Malaysia (1:500,000)

Authoritative national geoscientific dataset from the Department of Mineral and Geoscience Malaysia (Jabatan Mineral dan Geosains Malaysia - JMG) prepared for upstream integration into UW-Macrostrat (University of Wisconsin–Madison).

● ARTIFACTS PR-READY SCALE: 1:500,000 14 STATES COVERED EPSG:4326 / EPSG:3857 MYGEMS OPEN DATA

“Macrostrat's global model of the Earth's upper crust is the cornerstone of open paleogeographic intelligence. Bridging the 1:500,000 national bedrock of Malaysia into Macrostrat upgrades Southeast Asia from generalized 1:25M global coverage to authoritative, survey-grade stratigraphy across Sundaland and Northwest Borneo.”

— Muhammad Arif bin Fazil · Exploration Geoscientist & arifOS Federation Architect
