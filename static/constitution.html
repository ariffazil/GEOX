01 The Four Epistemic Labels

Every claim GEOX emits is tagged with one of four labels. The label determines how the claim is allowed to move, what it can authorize, and when it must be HOLD-ed. This is the doctrine of no naked Earth claims.

OBS

Observation

What was measured or recorded. The only label permitted to ground a claim.

e.g. GR curve exists 1200–2800 m MD
DER

Derivation

What was computed from validated observations. Deterministic outputs of physics.

e.g. Porosity from density log, Archie Sw
INT

Interpretation

What was concluded from OBS+DER. Always requires evidence, alternatives, and uncertainty.

e.g. Interval may be clean sand; net reservoir candidate
SPEC

Speculation

What was assumed — explicitly. Never promoted silently. Carries a SEAL gate.

e.g. Migration pathway, fluid phase assumption
