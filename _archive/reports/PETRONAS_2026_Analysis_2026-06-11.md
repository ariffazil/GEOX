## 2. TERSIRAT — Apa yang antara baris

### 2A. Macro pattern: dua paksi, satu worldview

- **Paksi 1 (SEARAH):** PETRONAS + Eni park big swath gas assets dalam UK satellite, run unified performance culture, financing, procurement. London jadi legal hub.
- **Paksi 2 (EnQuest/TI EP):** PETRONAS shed operatorship/capex mature PSCs ke mid-cap foreign + state JV. PETRONAS jadi portfolio governor, bukan operator.

**Tersirat:** PETRONAS shifts dari "operate everything ourselves" ke "govern capital and partners." Itu model yang semua NOC tengah migrate — bukan tanda kelemahan, tapi tanda evolutionary.

### 2B. Sovereignty & venue engineering

- **SEARAH:** Sarawak/NC3/Kasawari assets — yang duduk dalam unresolved MA63 + PETROS dispute domain — sekarang partly held via UK company governed by English law. Disputes = London, bukan Kuching/Sabah courts.
- **Tersirat:** Ini functionally short-circuits PETROS' natural forum. Apa-apa challenge kena route melalui English law + London arbitration. Cost/complexity naik untuk Sarawak.
- **EnQuest deals:** Sarawak fields Balingian/SK8/D35 — 100% operated UK-listed mid-cap (EnQuest). PETROS conspicuously absent.
- **PM6/12:** state participation pergi Terengganu Inc JV (TI EP). Bukan PETROS. Bukan Sabah. Peninsular side, bukan Borneo side.

**CLAIM:** Structure patterns tunjuk PETRONAS bagi lebih operational/equity doors ke foreign majors/mid-caps dan Peninsular-aligned state vehicles (TI EP) daripada Sarawak's own PETROS — despite 62-year MA63 dispute background.

### 2C. Governance asymmetry

- **PETRONAS board:** Appointed by federal government (sole shareholder, regulator, energy policy setter). Independence dari management, bukan dari political owner.
- **SEARAH board:** 2 Itali + 2 Malaysia. Physical asymmetry favours Eni (on-site, ENI House) vs Malaysians flying in. Tiada disclosed deadlock breaker.
- **Tersirat:** Dalam emergency atau contested decision, Eni likely ada structural advantage dalam presence dan process.
- **Parliament & states:** Tiada bukti Parliament formally briefed on SEARAH terms, despite multi-billion stakes dan Sarawak/Sabah implications. PETROS absent dari SEARAH equity dan dari EnQuest Sarawak handover.

**CLAIM:** System incentive optimum untuk institutional survival of PETRONAS + federal executive, bukan necessarily rakyat-level energy security atau state rights. SEARAH + EnQuest/TI EP fit structural bias tu.
